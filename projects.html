projects

Research themes across solid Earth geophysics, polar science, instrumentation, and water-related systems.

Continental structure

Imaging the crust and uppermost mantle to understand continental evolution, tectonics, and the physical state of the lithosphere.

Polar geophysics

Studying polar regions with geophysical tools to connect solid Earth structure, ice, climate, and long-term environmental change.

Extreme-condition instrumentation

Developing and deploying geophysical instrumentation for harsh, remote, and logistically difficult field environments.

Hydrogeology

Applying geophysical methods to questions involving groundwater, near-surface structure, and water-related hazards.
