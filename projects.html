projects

I work on Earth problems that are too deep, too cold, too remote, or too messy to observe directly. So we listen carefully.

Continental structure

What is the continent made of, and what has it survived? I image the crust and uppermost mantle to understand tectonics, lithosphere evolution, and the physical state of the solid Earth.

Polar geophysics

Ice sheets sit on geology. Geology pushes back. I study polar regions to connect solid Earth structure, ice, climate, and long-term change.

Extreme-condition instrumentation

Some instruments work beautifully in the lab. Then Antarctica introduces itself. I build and deploy geophysical systems for harsh and remote field conditions.

Hydrogeology

Water moves through the shallow Earth, often with consequences. I use geophysical tools to study groundwater, near-surface structure, and water-related hazards.
