Associate Professor · Stony Brook University

Weisen Shen

I study Earth structure and processes with geophysical observations, computation, and field instrumentation, with a broad interest in how geoscience helps society understand risk, resources, and our relationship with the planet.

Dr. Shen received a B.S. in geophysics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2008 and a Ph.D. in geophysics from the University of Colorado Boulder in 2014. He served as an Assistant Professor at Stony Brook University from 2018 to 2024 and is now an Associate Professor at Stony Brook University.

projects

Continental structure

Imaging the crust and uppermost mantle to understand continental evolution, tectonics, and the physical state of the lithosphere.

Polar geophysics

Studying polar regions with geophysical tools to connect solid Earth structure, ice, climate, and long-term environmental change.

Extreme-condition instrumentation

Developing and deploying geophysical instrumentation for harsh, remote, and logistically difficult field environments.

Hydrogeology

Applying geophysical methods to questions involving groundwater, near-surface structure, and water-related hazards.
