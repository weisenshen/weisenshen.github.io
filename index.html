Associate Professor · Stony Brook University

Weisen Shen

I use geophysics to see what we cannot see directly: crust, mantle, ice, water, and occasionally the assumptions hiding inside our models.

I am an Associate Professor at Stony Brook University. I work on continental structure, polar geophysics, instruments for places that do not care about your comfort, and water in the shallow Earth.

I received a B.S. in geophysics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2008 and a Ph.D. in geophysics from CU Boulder in 2014. I was an Assistant Professor at Stony Brook from 2018 to 2024.

I teach geophysics, structural geology, inverse theory, natural hazards, and the ways Earth science runs into society, finance, and civic life. Science is not separate from people. That would be convenient. It is also false.
