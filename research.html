Theme 2018 - present

Polar Geophysics

My field program deploys seismic and magnetotelluric instruments across Antarctica to characterize the solid Earth beneath the ice: bedrock geology, crustal structure, geothermal heat flux, and subglacial hydrology.

South Pole and East Antarctica

A large-scale seismic array near the South Pole has produced new constraints on crustal structure and sub-ice geology, including evidence for a large-scale subglacial aquifer system.

Manuscript in preparation · NSF CAREER

Geothermal Heat Flux

I produced the first seismology-derived geothermal heat flux map of Antarctica, now used as a community reference dataset in ice-sheet modeling and basal melt-rate estimation.

Shen et al., 2020 · Geophysical Research Letters

Ross Region and Dry Valleys

Planned projects in the Ross region and McMurdo Dry Valleys would combine seismic methods, ocean acoustic sensing, and co-located geophysical and geochemical characterization.

Under planning
Theme 2011 - present

Near-Surface Imaging and Bayesian Inversion

I develop and apply Bayesian joint inversion methods that combine multiple seismic observables: surface wave dispersion, receiver functions, Rayleigh wave ellipticity, H-k stacking, and Monte Carlo workflows. The goal is not just a model, but a model with honest uncertainty.

From velocity to composition and temperature

Work with Suyu Sui translates seismic properties into constraints on crustal composition and thermal state, including applications to Antarctica and the conterminous United States.

Sui et al., 2022 · Sui et al., 2025

Combining more observables

New methods led by Hanxiao Wu incorporate additional techniques into joint inversion workflows, including H-k stacking and Monte Carlo inversion of multiple seismic observables.

Wu et al., 2025
Theme Emerging

Groundwater and Environmental Geophysics

Sub-ice geological surveys in Antarctica suggest that groundwater can matter even in places where the surface says ice, not water. That motivates a broader program using seismic velocity, geodetic loading signals, electromagnetic sounding, and field monitoring to constrain groundwater storage and movement.

Long Island Hydrogeological Network

This developing program connects Flax Pond, Suffolk County Water Authority conversations, expanded monitoring sites, groundwater quality, saltwater intrusion, and public-facing maps and reports.

Developing observatory and public-impact program
Theme Emerging

Earth Systems and AI

I am interested in computational workflows that connect geophysical observations, public datasets, machine learning, uncertainty, and decision support. The work is practical: build tools that help people reason more clearly about Earth systems.

I am not a climate scientist. My work helps the climate research community better understand the physical conditions and processes that are critical to their work.
