Education & Mentoring

My educational work trains students to understand Earth systems through field observation, data, computation, instrumentation, and public relevance. Beginning in Fall 2026, I am building a connected set of courses, project teams, and open educational resources that support classroom teaching, research training, and broader public learning.

Vision

Educational Vision

Open by design

Courses should produce reusable, citable Earth-science materials: notes, notebooks, datasets, workflows, and examples that outlive a single semester.

Courses as living resources

A course is not a fixed packet of lectures. It is an evolving educational resource that improves as students, data, instruments, and public questions change.

Access to modern Earth science

Students should learn with the tools that modern Earth scientists actually use: sensors, maps, code, models, uncertainty, and public-facing products.

Courses

Courses and Course Development

GEO 542: Machine Learning & Inverse Theory in Earth Sciences

Quantitative inference for Earth systems: inverse problems, model uncertainty, machine learning, and the discipline of asking data what it can actually tell us.

ESS 542: Tectonic Environments

A graduate-level view of tectonic settings, lithospheric structure, and the physical processes that shape continents, margins, basins, and mountain belts.

Future: Surface Processes & Geohazards

A course connecting landscapes, water, sediment, hazards, field observation, and decision-making in a changing environment.

Future: Earth Science, Data, Policy & Society

Earth science does not stop at the edge of a map. This course will connect data, natural resources, risk, finance, policy, and civic life.

PEET

Vertically Integrated Project Team

PEET is a vertically integrated project where undergraduate students work alongside graduate students and faculty on instrumentation, sensing, mapping, and Earth exploration technologies. It is not merely a course; it is a training ground for students who want to build things that can survive outside the classroom.

The team connects mentoring, open learning, field systems, and public-facing products. Fiona Clerc and Deanna Rogers are part of the developing PEET education and mentoring ecosystem.

ARGOS

Instrumentation Facility

ARGOS is an enabling platform: instrumentation, sensors, drones, GPR, seismic nodes, fabrication, and field systems that support both teaching and research. Under education, it gives students access to the tools and habits of modern field science.
